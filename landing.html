    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Quick answers about booking, payments, queueing and tournaments.

    Do I need an account to reserve a court?

    You can browse the courts, open hours and booked slots without one. To book, you need to sign up and log in.

    How do I reserve a court?

    Log in, choose a court, pick a date and hour, then pay by scanning the payment QR shown during booking or pay directly to the counter. Staff verify your payment before the booking is confirmed.

    Can I cancel or get a refund?

    Cash reservations can be cancelled. Once an online payment is made, the booking is not cancellable or refundable as a rule. The owner may consider exceptions case by case, depending on the reason, so please double-check your date and time before paying.

    Is there a discount for homeowners?

    Yes. Homeowners of Southpoint Subdivision get a discounted court rate once their homeowner status is verified.

    What if all the courts are taken?

    Join the reservation queue for busy hours. It is first come, first served, and it shows your place in line and your estimated wait time, so you do not have to wait around on-site.

    How do tournaments and skill levels work?

    Register online when an event is open, then follow your bracket and see your next match as soon as the current game ends. Your level (beginner, intermediate or advanced) comes from recorded assessments, so everyone competes against players at their level.

    How does coaching work, and who do I pay?

    Request a coach and choose a single session or a package. Coaching fees are paid to the coach directly, separate from the court fee.

    What time are you open?

    Opening hours can differ by day. The current weekly hours are listed under Visit us.
